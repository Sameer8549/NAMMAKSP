# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Karnataka Police investigators, crime analysts, supervisors, policymakers, and platform administrators using role-specific workspaces during repeated operational analysis and prototype evaluation.

## Product Purpose

NAMMA KSP is a conversational crime-intelligence and analytics workspace. It connects FIRs, offenders, victims, locations, relationships, financial signals, and socio-economic indicators to support investigation, analysis, supervision, prevention planning, and accountable governance.

## Positioning

The product combines bilingual natural-language investigation with evidence-linked crime analytics, network analysis, role-aware disclosure, explainability, and operational reports in one police workflow.

## Operating Context

Users scan statewide and district signals, query cases, inspect networks and hotspots, review offender risk, generate reports, and trace audit evidence. The finale prototype runs on Zoho Catalyst and must remain reliable under live judging.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five server-enforced roles with distinct disclosure policies.
- English and Kannada chat, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and translation.
- Synthetic demonstration datasets only; never imply live KSP/CCTNS connectivity.
- Preserve all backend API contracts, PDF rendering, QR behavior, report downloads, and Catalyst integrations.
- Operational usability, accessibility, responsive behavior, and evidence clarity outrank decoration.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the product name NAMMA KSP, Karnataka State Police identity, official emblem assets, English/Kannada support, and a professional law-enforcement voice.

## Evidence on Hand

Synthetic FIR, offender, victim, location, relationship, financial transaction, and socio-economic datasets; functioning local application; Catalyst service configuration; generated report evidence; official product logo assets.

## Product Principles

- Evidence before assertion.
- Role and purpose determine disclosure.
- Dense information must remain calm and scannable.
- Every warning should lead to a concrete investigative action.
- Degraded services must fail honestly and recover clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, contrast-compliant light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and legible Kannada typography.
